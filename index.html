QoL Add-on Builder

Tap the features you want, then download a pack with just those. Everything stays vanilla: no new items, blocks or recipes. The pack is built in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Rebuilding? Drop an old QoL .mcaddon here (or ) to pick the same features again.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded .mcaddon. Minecraft starts and imports it.
  2. Create or edit a world, go to Behavior Packs, and activate QoL Add-on.
  3. Optional: tap the pack's gear to switch features off for the whole world or lower the limits (world owner only).
  4. Updating: after importing a new version, open the world's Behavior Packs again and re-activate it. Worlds remember the old version and won't load the new one until you do.
  1. Set the pack up on a world on PC first (see the PC tab).
  2. Upload that world to the Realm: Realm settings, Replace World.
  3. Switch, Xbox and phone players get the pack automatically when they join. Nothing to install on the console.
  4. Each update: bump the version, re-activate on the PC world, and upload again. Same version number = consoles keep the old cached copy.

Menu: sneak + jump while standing on the ground. Works from the first minute, no items needed. A clock in your hand works too.

Your settings: every feature can be switched off or tuned per player in the menu's Settings. One player's choices never change anyone else's.

Controls: sneak is Shift on PC, pushing in the right stick on Switch (or the sneak button if you set one). "Use" is right click / ZL.

Achievements: Minecraft turns them off in any world with an imported behavior pack. That's the game, not this pack.
